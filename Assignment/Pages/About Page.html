About Richfield Connect

Richfield Connect is a platform designed to provide a simple way for students to create profiles, share updates and interact with their student community.

Our Purpose

The purpose of Richfield Connect - to provide a platform for students to connect, share and interact with their peers.

What You Can Do

  • Create and Customise your student profile.
  • Share posts with the community.
  • Interact with posts using likes.
  • Manage your own posts.
